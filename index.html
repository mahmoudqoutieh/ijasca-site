What we do

Three services, one standard of rigor

From the first editorial screen to the final DOI-ready article, every step is handled by people with academic training.

Manuscript evaluation

An editor reads every submission for scope, methodology, and ethics before it goes to review, so reviewers' time is spent on work that is ready.

Double-blind peer review

At least two independent experts assess each paper without knowing who wrote it. You receive their full comments, not just a verdict.

Professional publishing

Copyediting, typesetting, and online publication with a permanent article page, so your work is easy to find, cite, and share.

Editorial workflow

How a manuscript moves from submission to publication

Typical times are shown for each stage. Revisions requested by reviewers add to the total.

  1. Day 0

    Submission

    You send the anonymized manuscript and a cover letter.

  2. 3–5 days

    Editorial screening

    Scope, formatting, and a similarity check.

  3. 3–5 weeks

    Peer review

    Two or more blind reviewers submit detailed reports.

  4. As needed

    Decision & revision

    Accept, revise, or reject, with reasons in writing.

  5. 1–2 weeks

    Publication

    Copyediting, proofs for your approval, then online release.

Our rigorous double-blind peer-review process upholds high standards of academic integrity, originality, and quality. Our publishing services are professional and efficient, giving scholarly work broad visibility and accessibility.

We work with early-career researchers publishing their first paper as well as established faculty and research groups. Many of our authors write in English as a second language, and our editors are used to helping clear, careful science come through in clear, careful prose.

How to submit

Send your files by email

Email your manuscript to the editorial office. Put the article type and title in the subject line, for example: Research Article – Deep Learning for Crop Yield Prediction.

submissions@ijasca.net

You will receive an acknowledgement with a manuscript ID within two working days.

What to attach

  • Anonymized manuscript (.docx) with no author names, affiliations, or self-identifying references.
  • Title page as a separate file: authors, affiliations, ORCID iDs, and the corresponding author's email.
  • Cover letter explaining the contribution and confirming the work is not under review elsewhere.
  • Figures and data at print resolution (300 dpi or higher), if not embedded.

Formatting

Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing, numbered pages and line numbers, A4 or US Letter.

Language

Manuscripts must be in English. Papers with serious language problems are returned before review.

Originality

A similarity index above 20% (excluding references) leads to return or rejection without review.

Fees

Article Processing Charge

Submission and peer review are free. An Article Processing Charge (APC) is payable only after your paper is accepted. It covers copyediting, typesetting, hosting, and long-term access.

The exact APC is stated in your acceptance letter and invoice. Fee waivers or discounts may be available for authors from low-income countries and for students; request one at submission.

Refunds are governed by our Refund Policy.

Questions

Frequently asked

How long does peer review take?

Most manuscripts receive a first decision within 4–6 weeks of submission. Complex or specialized papers can take longer; we will let you know if they do.

Can I submit a paper that is under review elsewhere?

No. Simultaneous submission to more than one journal or publisher is not permitted.

Who owns the copyright?

You do. Authors grant IJASCA Publications a license to publish and distribute the article under an open-access license.

Can I suggest or exclude reviewers?

Yes. List them in your cover letter with reasons. The editor makes the final choice.

What if my paper is rejected?

You receive the reviewers' comments so you can improve the work. No APC is charged for rejected papers.

IJASCA Publications ("we", "us", "our") respects the privacy of authors, reviewers, editors, and visitors. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and editorial services and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

  • Authors: names, affiliations, email addresses, ORCID iDs, and the content of submitted manuscripts.
  • Reviewers and editors: names, contact details, academic credentials, areas of expertise, and review reports.
  • Billing: names, institutional billing addresses, and invoice records. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Website visitors: basic technical data such as browser type, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from IP address.

2. How we use information

  • To process, review, and publish manuscripts.
  • To communicate with authors and reviewers about submissions.
  • To issue invoices and process payments and refunds.
  • To detect plagiarism, fraud, and ethics violations.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Peer-review confidentiality

Under our double-blind process, author identities are withheld from reviewers and reviewer identities are withheld from authors. Unpublished manuscripts are confidential and are shared only with the editors and reviewers handling them.

4. Sharing information

We share personal information only when needed to provide our services: with reviewers (manuscript content only), with service providers such as hosting, email, similarity-checking, and payment processors, with indexing services when an article is published (author names and affiliations), or when required by law.

5. Retention and security

We keep submission records for as long as needed to maintain the scholarly record and meet legal obligations. Published author information is retained permanently as part of the article. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Some information in published articles cannot be removed without affecting the scholarly record. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to function and analytics cookies to understand how it is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@ijasca.net. We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Refund Policy

When Article Processing Charges can be refunded, and how to request one.

1. Overview

Submission and peer review at IJASCA Publications are free. The only charge is the Article Processing Charge (APC), which is invoiced after acceptance. Because the APC pays for work done after acceptance, whether a refund is possible depends on how far production has progressed.

2. Refund by stage

SituationRefund
Payment made in error, or a duplicate payment100%
Withdrawal after payment, before copyediting begins100%, minus payment-processor fees
Withdrawal after copyediting begins, before proofs are sent50%
Withdrawal after proofs are sent or after publicationNo refund
We are unable to publish the accepted article for reasons on our side100%

3. Non-refundable cases

  • The article is retracted or withdrawn because of plagiarism, data fabrication, duplicate submission, or other author misconduct.
  • The author is dissatisfied with the review outcome or the time taken, where the published timelines were followed.
  • The article has already been published online.

4. How to request a refund

The corresponding author should email billing@ijasca.net with the manuscript ID, invoice number, payment date, and the reason for the request. Requests must be made within 30 days of payment.

5. Processing time

We review requests within 7 working days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 10–15 working days. Bank or currency-conversion charges may apply and are outside our control.
